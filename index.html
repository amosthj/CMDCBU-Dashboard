    Each rate uses its own scale. Dashed line is the 2026 target.

    WIRInjuries per 100,000 workers
    AFRReportable accidents per 1,000,000 man-hours
    ASRMan-days lost per 1,000,000 man-hours
    WIR
    Workplace Injury Rate = (fatal + non-fatal workplace injuries ÷ average workers employed) × 100,000
    AFR
    Accident Frequency Rate = (reportable accidents ÷ man-hours worked) × 1,000,000
    ASR
    Accident Severity Rate = (man-days lost ÷ man-hours worked) × 1,000,000
